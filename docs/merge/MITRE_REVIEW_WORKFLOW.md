# MITRE Review Workflow

AIIE technique output is persisted only as a `PROPOSED` mapping with its AIIE item and copied factual support. It is never an analyst-confirmed technique automatically.

The MITRE workspace separates `PROPOSED`, `CONFIRMED`, and `REJECTED` mappings. Analysts may confirm a proposal or reject it with a required rationale; the reviewer, timestamp, rationale, and audit event are recorded. Supporting fact links remain available for every mapping.
